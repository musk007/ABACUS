**Morphologic interpretation:** The myeloblast cohort shows a strongly conserved immature nuclear pattern, with uniformly open chromatin, nearly universal prominent nucleoli, and strongly predominant irregular nuclear contours. Predominantly medium cell size and consistently moderate cytoplasmic basophilia accompany this nuclear immaturity. Cytoplasmic volume is more variable, with scant cytoplasm only weakly dominant, whereas cytoplasmic vacuolation is uniformly absent.

**Diagnostic flags:** blasts present; blast threshold met.

**Impression:** Findings consistent with an acute leukaemia of myeloblastic morphology. Definitive classification requires clinical correlation and ancillary studies.

Myeloblasts comprise 88.5% of WBCs (blast-equivalent burden of 88.5%), exceeding the 20% blast threshold for acute leukaemia.

**Differential considerations:**

**Recommended workup:**

- Bone marrow aspirate and trephine biopsy.
- Cytogenetics and FISH for recurrent AML abnormalities.
- Molecular panel: NPM1, FLT3-ITD/TKD, CEBPA, RUNX1, TP53, and AML fusion screen.
