**Morphologic interpretation:** The lymphoblast cohort shows a strongly predominant medium-sized, scant-cytoplasm pattern with frequent nuclear contour irregularity. Uniformly coarse nuclear chromatin and predominantly inconspicuous nucleoli create a relatively condensed nuclear appearance rather than an overtly nucleolated blast pattern. Uniformly slight cytoplasmic basophilia and absent vacuolation argue against a deeply basophilic, vacuolated morphologic pattern.

**Impression:** Findings consistent with an acute leukaemia of lymphoblastic morphology. Definitive classification requires clinical correlation and ancillary studies.

Lymphoblasts comprise 71.4% of WBCs (blast-equivalent burden of 71.4%), exceeding the 20% blast threshold for acute leukaemia.

**Differential considerations:**

- Mixed-phenotype acute leukaemia (excluded by immunophenotyping).

**Recommended workup:**
