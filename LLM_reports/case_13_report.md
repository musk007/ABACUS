**Morphologic interpretation:** The lymphoblast cohort shows a predominantly small-cell pattern with strongly predominant scant cytoplasm and uniformly irregular nuclear contours. Nearly uniform open nuclear chromatin supports morphologic immaturity despite uniformly inconspicuous nucleoli and predominantly small cell size. Uniformly slight cytoplasmic basophilia and absent vacuolation further characterize a restrained cytoplasmic pattern rather than a deeply basophilic, vacuolated appearance.

**Diagnostic flags:** blasts present; blast threshold met.

**Impression:** Findings consistent with an acute leukaemia of lymphoblastic morphology. Definitive classification requires clinical correlation and ancillary studies.
