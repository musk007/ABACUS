**Cohort morphology (n = 197 atypical lymphocytes):** predominantly medium cells (99.5%) with abundant (100.0%), slightly basophilic (100.0%) cytoplasm and absent cytoplasmic vacuolation (100.0%); nuclei show coarse chromatin (100.0%), irregular contours (99.5%), and inconspicuous nucleoli (100.0%).

**Morphologic interpretation:** The atypical lymphocyte cohort shows a highly uniform pattern of medium cell size and irregular nuclear contours, with consistently abundant, slightly basophilic cytoplasm. Uniformly coarse nuclear chromatin and inconspicuous nucleoli argue against a blast-like or prominent-nucleolar pattern despite the nuclear contour abnormalities. The combination of nuclear irregularity with abundant cytoplasm characterizes the atypia without establishing a specific lymphoid entity.

**Diagnostic flags:** atypical lymphocytes present.

**Impression:** Findings consistent with a mature lymphoid proliferation. Definitive classification requires clinical correlation and ancillary studies.

The smear is dominated by atypical mature lymphocytes (92.9% of informative WBCs) and no significant blast population.

**Differential considerations:**
- Chronic lymphocytic leukaemia / small lymphocytic lymphoma.
- Mantle cell lymphoma in leukemic phase (excluded by CD5+/CD23+/cyclin D1- profile).
- Prolymphocytic leukemia (>55% prolymphocytes).
- Reactive lymphocytosis (e.g. viral) — typically polyclonal.

**Recommended workup:**
- Flow cytometric immunophenotyping (CLL panel: CD5, CD19, CD20, CD23, CD79b, FMC7, kappa/lambda light chain restriction).
- Matutes / CLL score calculation.
- FISH panel: del(13q), del(11q), del(17p)/TP53, trisomy 12.
- IGHV mutation status and TP53 sequencing (prognostic and predictive).
- Beta-2 microglobulin and standard staging workup (Rai/Binet).

**QC:** 63 FOVs; 212/260 cells classifiable (18.5% artefact); cohort cell count = 197.

**Not assessed:** erythrocyte and platelet morphology, dysplastic features, smear staining quality, and whether fields originate from the optimal monolayer zone. Findings are based on leukocyte morphology only.
