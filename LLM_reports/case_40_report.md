**Morphologic interpretation:** The promonocyte cohort shows a cohesive pattern of uniformly irregular nuclei and abundant, moderately basophilic cytoplasm with prominent vacuolation. Strongly predominant open nuclear chromatin supports nuclear immaturity despite predominantly inconspicuous nucleoli. The combination of these nuclear features with abundant vacuolated cytoplasm supports immature monocytic morphology in a predominantly medium-sized cohort.

**Diagnostic flags:** blasts present; left shifted myeloid; monocytosis present.

**Impression:** Findings consistent with an acute leukaemia showing monocytic differentiation. Definitive classification requires clinical correlation and ancillary studies.

Monocytic-lineage predominance (promonocytes 17.6%) with a blast-equivalent burden of 8.8% is suspicious for a monocytic AML pattern under sparse annotation conditions.

**Differential considerations:**
- Acute monoblastic/monocytic leukaemia.
- Acute myelomonocytic leukaemia.
- Reactive monocytosis with immature forms (less likely).

**Recommended workup:**
- Flow cytometric immunophenotyping (CD64, CD14, CD11b, lysozyme, CD33).
- Bone marrow aspirate and trephine biopsy.
- Cytogenetics and targeted molecular AML panel.
