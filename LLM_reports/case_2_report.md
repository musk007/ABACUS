**Cohort morphology (n = 39 lymphoblasts):** predominantly medium cells (94.9%) with scanty (61.5%), slightly basophilic (100.0%) cytoplasm and absent cytoplasmic vacuolation (92.3%); nuclei show coarse chromatin (97.4%), irregular contours (94.9%), and inconspicuous nucleoli (76.9%).

**Morphologic interpretation:** The lymphoblast cohort shows a highly consistent pattern of medium size, irregular nuclear contours, and coarse nuclear chromatin, with predominantly inconspicuous nucleoli producing a relatively condensed rather than open, nucleolated nuclear appearance. Scant cytoplasm is only weakly dominant, indicating less uniformity in cytoplasmic volume than in the principal nuclear features. Uniformly slight cytoplasmic basophilia and the strong predominance of absent vacuolation argue against a deeply basophilic, conspicuously vacuolated morphologic pattern.

**Diagnostic flags:** blasts present; blast threshold met.

**Impression:** Findings consistent with an acute leukaemia of lymphoblastic morphology. Definitive classification requires clinical correlation and ancillary studies.

Lymphoblasts comprise 63.9% of WBCs (blast-equivalent burden of 63.9%), exceeding the 20% blast threshold for acute leukaemia.

**Differential considerations:**
- B- or T-lymphoblastic leukaemia/lymphoma.
- Mixed-phenotype acute leukaemia (excluded by immunophenotyping).

**Recommended workup:**
- Flow cytometric immunophenotyping for lineage assignment (B-ALL: CD19, CD22, CD79a, CD10, TdT; T-ALL: cytoCD3, CD7, TdT).
- Bone marrow aspirate and trephine biopsy.
- Cytogenetics and FISH for recurrent ALL abnormalities (BCR::ABL1, KMT2A rearrangements, ETV6::RUNX1, TCF3::PBX1, hypodiploidy).
- Molecular studies including BCR::ABL1-like signature where available.
- CSF examination to assess CNS involvement.
