Uniformly slight cytoplasmic basophilia and absent vacuolation establish a consistent cytoplasmic character despite variation in cytoplasmic volume.

**Impression:** Findings consistent with an acute leukaemia of lymphoblastic morphology. Definitive classification requires clinical correlation and ancillary studies.

Lymphoblasts comprise 78.3% of WBCs (blast-equivalent burden of 78.3%), exceeding the 20% blast threshold for acute leukaemia.

**Differential considerations:**

- Mixed-phenotype acute leukaemia (excluded by immunophenotyping).

**Recommended workup:**

- Cytogenetics and FISH for recurrent ALL abnormalities (BCR::ABL1, KMT2A rearrangements, ETV6::RUNX1, TCF3::PBX1, hypodiploidy).
