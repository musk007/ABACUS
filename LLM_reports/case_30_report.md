**Cohort morphology (n = 36 myeloblasts):** predominantly medium cells (94.4%) with abundant (55.6%), moderately basophilic (97.2%) cytoplasm and absent cytoplasmic vacuolation (100.0%); nuclei show open chromatin (100.0%), irregular contours (97.2%), and prominent nucleoli (97.2%).

**Morphologic interpretation:** The myeloblast cohort shows a cohesive immature nuclear pattern, with uniformly open chromatin and strongly predominant prominent nucleoli and irregular nuclear contours in predominantly medium-sized cells. Cytoplasmic abundance is only weakly dominant, indicating variable cytoplasmic volume despite the consistent nuclear immaturity. Moderate cytoplasmic basophilia is strongly predominant and vacuolation is uniformly absent, completing a relatively consistent cytoplasmic staining pattern despite variation in volume.

**Diagnostic flags:** blasts present; left shifted myeloid.

**Impression:** Findings consistent with a chronic myeloproliferative process with left-shifted granulopoiesis. Definitive classification requires clinical correlation and ancillary studies.

Left-shifted granulocytic series with the full spectrum of maturation (myelocytes, metamyelocytes, neutrophils), and a blast-equivalent burden of 8.3% (below the 20% threshold for blast phase) is compatible with a chronic myeloproliferative pattern.

**Differential considerations:**
- Chronic myeloid leukaemia — requires BCR::ABL1 confirmation.
- Leukemoid reaction or severe infection/sepsis, particularly if basophils are absent.
- Other MPN (PV, ET, primary myelofibrosis).
- Atypical CML, BCR::ABL1 negative.
- CML in accelerated or blast phase (excluded by blast count and morphology).

**Recommended workup:**
- BCR::ABL1 testing by RT-PCR (qualitative and quantitative) — diagnostic.
- FISH for t(9;22) / Philadelphia chromosome.
- Bone marrow aspirate and biopsy with cytogenetics.
- Baseline transcript level for IS-standardized monitoring during TKI therapy.
- Sokal / EUTOS / ELTS risk score calculation at diagnosis.
