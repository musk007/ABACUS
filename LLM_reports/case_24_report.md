**Cohort morphology (n = 212 myeloblasts):** predominantly medium cells (67.0%) with abundant (98.6%), moderately basophilic (99.5%) cytoplasm and absent cytoplasmic vacuolation (100.0%); nuclei show open chromatin (100.0%), irregular contours (100.0%), and prominent nucleoli (90.6%).

**Morphologic interpretation:** The myeloblast cohort shows a strongly expressed immature nuclear pattern, combining uniformly open chromatin and irregular nuclear contours with predominantly prominent nucleoli. Nearly universal abundant, moderately basophilic cytoplasm accompanies this nuclear immaturity, rather than a scant-cytoplasm blast pattern; cytoplasmic vacuolation is uniformly absent. Medium cell size predominates less strongly than the nuclear and cytoplasmic attributes, indicating size variation within an otherwise morphologically consistent cohort.

**Diagnostic flags:** blasts present; blast threshold met; abnormal promyelocytes present.

**Impression:** Findings consistent with an acute leukaemia showing abnormal promyelocyte morphology. Definitive classification requires clinical correlation and ancillary studies.

Abnormal promyelocytes comprise 34.0% of WBCs, with a blast-equivalent burden of 97.3%, meeting criteria for acute leukaemia.

**Differential considerations:**
- Acute promyelocytic leukaemia — requires urgent PML::RARA confirmation.
- AML, not otherwise specified (less likely given promyelocyte morphology).

**Recommended workup:**
- URGENT: PML::RARA fusion testing (RT-PCR or FISH) — APL is a medical emergency requiring immediate ATRA initiation on clinical suspicion.
- Flow cytometric immunophenotyping (typically CD33+, CD13+, CD117+/-, HLA-DR negative, CD34 negative).
- Coagulation panel (PT/PTT, fibrinogen, D-dimer) to assess for DIC.
- Bone marrow aspirate and biopsy for confirmation.
- Cytogenetics for t(15;17) and screening for variant RARA translocations.
