**Morphologic interpretation:** The promonocyte cohort shows a highly uniform immature nuclear pattern, with open chromatin and prominent nucleoli throughout, coupled with consistently irregular nuclear contours. Nearly uniform medium cell size and uniformly abundant, moderately basophilic cytoplasm establish a coherent nuclear–cytoplasmic pattern supportive of immature monocytic morphology. Prominent cytoplasmic vacuolation is strongly predominant but not universal, adding cytoplasmic heterogeneity to the otherwise uniform pattern.

**Diagnostic flags:** blasts present; left shifted myeloid.

**Impression:** Findings consistent with an acute leukaemia showing monocytic differentiation. Definitive classification requires clinical correlation and ancillary studies.

Monocytic-lineage predominance (promonocytes 84.0%) with a blast-equivalent burden of 8.6% is suspicious for a monocytic AML pattern under sparse annotation conditions.

**Differential considerations:**
- Acute monoblastic/monocytic leukaemia.
- Acute myelomonocytic leukaemia.
- Reactive monocytosis with immature forms (less likely).

**Recommended workup:**
- Flow cytometric immunophenotyping (CD64, CD14, CD11b, lysozyme, CD33).
- Bone marrow aspirate and trephine biopsy.
- Cytogenetics and targeted molecular AML panel.
