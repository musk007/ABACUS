**Morphologic interpretation:** The lymphoblast cohort shows nearly uniform coarse nuclear chromatin and scant cytoplasm with uniformly inconspicuous nucleoli, indicating a relatively condensed rather than open-chromatin, nucleolated pattern. Cell size is variable, with medium size only weakly dominant over small cells. Irregular nuclear contours predominate but are less consistent than the chromatin and cytoplasmic features. Uniformly slight cytoplasmic basophilia and absent vacuolation reinforce a scant, nonvacuolated cytoplasmic pattern.

**Diagnostic flags:** blasts present; blast threshold met; eosinophilia present.

**Impression:** Findings consistent with an acute leukaemia of lymphoblastic morphology. Definitive classification requires clinical correlation and ancillary studies.

Lymphoblasts comprise 40.4% of WBCs (blast-equivalent burden of 40.4%), exceeding the 20% blast threshold for acute leukaemia.

**Differential considerations:**
- B- or T-lymphoblastic leukaemia/lymphoma.
- Mixed-phenotype acute leukaemia (excluded by immunophenotyping).

**Recommended workup:**
- Flow cytometric immunophenotyping for lineage assignment (B-ALL: CD19, CD22, CD79a, CD10, TdT; T-ALL: cytoCD3, CD7, TdT).
- Bone marrow aspirate and trephine biopsy.
- Cytogenetics and FISH for recurrent ALL abnormalities (BCR::ABL1, KMT2A rearrangements, ETV6::RUNX1, TCF3::PBX1, hypodiploidy).
- Molecular studies including BCR::ABL1-like signature where available.
- CSF examination to assess CNS involvement.
