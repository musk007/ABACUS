**Morphologic interpretation:** The lymphoblast cohort shows a strongly predominant small-cell pattern with nearly uniform scant cytoplasm and uniform nuclear irregularity. Uniformly open nuclear chromatin supports morphologic immaturity despite the small cell size and inconspicuous nucleoli. Uniform slight cytoplasmic basophilia and absent vacuolation further characterize a relatively consistent, cytoplasm-poor pattern rather than a deeply basophilic, vacuolated appearance.

**Diagnostic flags:** blasts present; blast threshold met.

**Impression:** Findings consistent with an acute leukaemia of lymphoblastic morphology. Definitive classification requires clinical correlation and ancillary studies.

Lymphoblasts comprise 96.9% of WBCs (blast-equivalent burden of 96.9%), exceeding the 20% blast threshold for acute leukaemia.

**Differential considerations:**

- Mixed-phenotype acute leukaemia (excluded by immunophenotyping).

**Recommended workup:**

- Cytogenetics and FISH for recurrent ALL abnormalities (BCR::ABL1, KMT2A rearrangements, ETV6::RUNX1, TCF3::PBX1, hypodiploidy).
