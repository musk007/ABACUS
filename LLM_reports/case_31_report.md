**Morphologic interpretation:** The myeloblast cohort shows a consistent immature nuclear pattern, with uniformly open chromatin, prominent nucleoli, and irregular nuclear contours in predominantly medium-sized cells. Abundant cytoplasm predominates but is less uniform than the nuclear features, indicating variation in cytoplasmic volume despite a shared immature nuclear appearance. Uniform moderate cytoplasmic basophilia and absent vacuolation further characterize the cohort without establishing a specific disease category.

**Diagnostic flags:** blasts present; left shifted myeloid.

**Impression:** Findings consistent with a chronic myeloproliferative process with left-shifted granulopoiesis. Definitive classification requires clinical correlation and ancillary studies.

Left-shifted granulocytic series with the full spectrum of maturation (myelocytes, metamyelocytes, neutrophils), and a blast-equivalent burden of 6.4% (below the 20% threshold for blast phase) is compatible with a chronic myeloproliferative pattern.

**Differential considerations:**
- Chronic myeloid leukaemia — requires BCR::ABL1 confirmation.
- Leukemoid reaction or severe infection/sepsis, particularly if basophils are absent.
- Other MPN (PV, ET, primary myelofibrosis).
- Atypical CML, BCR::ABL1 negative.
- CML in accelerated or blast phase (excluded by blast count and morphology).

**Recommended workup:**
- BCR::ABL1 testing by RT-PCR (qualitative and quantitative) — diagnostic.
- FISH for t(9;22) / Philadelphia chromosome.
- Bone marrow aspirate and biopsy with cytogenetics.
- Baseline transcript level for IS-standardized monitoring during TKI therapy.
- Sokal / EUTOS / ELTS risk score calculation at diagnosis.
